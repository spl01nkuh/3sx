CRT Lottes / zfast / CRT Caligari
--------------------------------
Primary sources: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders
Pinned source commit: 1aaabbebdd88a8a89a82edfdc94e10580c99ffdd

CRT Lottes is explicitly public domain, by Timothy Lottes.
zfast CRT: Copyright (C) 2017 Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), GPL-2.0-or-later.
CRT Caligari: Copyright (C) 2011 caligari; ported by Hyllian, GPL-2.0-or-later.

Unmodified source files, original notices and GPL text are retained in
third_party_sources/libretro-crt. The 3SX single-pass Metal adaptations use
Gaussian beams/curvature/RGB mask (Lottes), cubic scaling and adaptive scanlines
(zfast), and gamma-corrected phosphor spots (Caligari). These adaptations are
distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. See the retained ORIGIN.md for source links.

CRT Aperture / CRT Geom / CRT Hyllian
------------------------------------
Primary sources: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders
Pinned source commit: f277bd87e454a68c50e01e61e3588118d56b1365

CRT Aperture: CRT Shader by EasyMode. Header states "License: GPL".
CRT Geom: Copyright (C) 2010-2012 cgwg, Themaister and DOLLS,
GPL version 2 or later.
CRT Hyllian: Copyright (C) 2011-2020 Hyllian - sergiogdb@gmail.com,
MIT license. Its embedded mask collection is by hunterk, public domain.

Complete original source files and license headers are retained in
third_party_sources/libretro-crt. The single-pass Metal adaptations use
Gaussian halation and dual Lanczos scaling (Aperture), spherical glass geometry
and rounded corners (Geom), and Catmull-Rom anti-ringing reconstruction with
variable beam width (Hyllian). Existing filter IDs and Carlos' sources remain.
